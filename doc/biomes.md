# Biomes

Usually, biomes refers to the types of terrain found on a zone in the map. In Aetherships, biomes are the type of planets and asteroids you can find. A biome determines the appearance of the planet and the resources it contains.

Some biomes are more common than others, some can be for planets only, asteroids only, or both. The player can find biomes with different frequencies in different sectors of the galaxy, with some biomes being more common in certain regions.
